## Frictional log

`FRICTIONAL.md` in this folder is a weekly, dated record of the work: what was tried and
expected, where it resisted and what was done next, what Claude or another person
contributed and what was accepted, changed or rejected, and what is now understood or
still open. It is not graded and not a performance review — it is documentation of
practical learning, kept alongside the work it describes. See
<https://www.humanitarians.ai/fellows> for what an entry contains.
